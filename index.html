Happy Birthday, Vedika ❤️🎂,


I don't know if you'll ever truly understand how much you mean to me, but I hope that someday you realise just how special you are to me. And since it's your birthday, I wanted to write something from my heart, something that reminds you of how much you mean to me and how grateful I am that our paths crossed.


It's honestly crazy to think that everything started with CODM. What began as a game slowly turned into daily conversations, random talks, late-night calls, and eventually those calls where we'd fall asleep without even realising it. Somewhere along the way, you went from being someone I played games with to someone I genuinely looked forward to talking to every single day. I never expected a person I met through a game to become such an important part of my life, but somehow, you did.


There are so many little things about you that I love. Your hair, your eyes, your voice, your smile, and especially that smile you get after realising you've successfully ragebaited me.The way you manage to irritate me and make me smile at the same time is genuinely impressive and they're some of the things I'll always remember about you.


I miss our CODM matches, our late-night conversations, the random topics that somehow kept us talking for hours, and those sleepy calls where neither of us really wanted to hang up. Even the smallest moments became memories that mean something to me now. It's funny how ordinary things can become so special when they're shared with the right person.


I'm not going to pretend that everything has been easy or that I completely understand where life has taken us. Things have changed, and there are moments I wish had turned out differently. But despite everything, I'm still grateful that I got to know you. I got to know your silly side, your caring side, your annoying side, and all those little parts of you that made me grow so attached to you. And if I could go back to the day we first met, knowing everything I know now, I think I'd still want to meet you. Because even if I couldn't predict where we'd end up, meeting you gave me memories I'll always be thankful for.


Vedika, on your birthday, I genuinely wish you all the happiness this world has to offer. I hope you achieve everything you've ever dreamed of, that life gives you countless reasons to smile, and that you always have people around you who appreciate you for exactly who you are. I hope you take care of yourself, look after your health, never doubt your worth. You have so much ahead of you, and I really hope life treats you beautifully.


I hope this new year of your life brings you new experiences, unforgettable memories, lots of laughter, and the kind of happiness that stays even after the moment has passed. Keep being the person you are, keep smiling that beautiful smile of yours, and please never lose the little things that make you special. And maybe, just maybe, try ragebaiting me a little less and please stop saying hat to me. Although, knowing you, that's probably an impossible birthday wish.


I don't know what place I'll have in your life as time goes on, and I don't want to make your birthday about that. Today is about you, your happiness, and the wonderful person you are. Regardless of what the future holds for us, I want you to know that the time we've shared has meant a lot to me, and I'll always be grateful for the happiness and memories you brought into my life.


Thank you for being a part of my life, for all the laughs, the late-night talks, the memories, and even the little arguments that somehow became funny later. Thank you for being someone who made ordinary days feel a little more special. I'll always hope for the best for you, even as life takes us in whatever direction it chooses.I hope today makes you feel as special as you truly are. You deserve a beautiful birthday, a beautiful year ahead, and a life filled with reasons to be happy.


Happy Birthday once again, Vedika. Take care of yourself, keep that smile on your face, and never forget how precious you are.


I am so proud of you.


Happy birthday, my love.❤️


Have the most unforgettable and joyous birthday ever!

With all my love, Ayush
